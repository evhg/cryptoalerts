  • Standing withdrawal, in every block

    A withdrawal that waits inside each block and only goes through when USDS is withdrawable at that moment, so liquidity freed by a transaction nobody saw is caught in the same block. It costs nothing while it waits, and stays idle until the served wallet has stUSDS and a signed approval.

  • Standing arb, in every block

    An arb that also waits inside each block, sizes itself when it runs and takes whatever is withdrawable after your withdrawal.

    —
  • Arb after the exit

    When liquidity is left over, buys discounted stUSDS with a flash loan, redeems it and sends the profit to the main wallet. In an open race it currently offers the block builder — of the profit; that share drops a little after each win and rises after each loss.     A Sky governance switch, outside our control. While it is off, under-collateralised loans are not sold off.
